It was six o'clock on a Friday night. Maria was getting ready for her date at seven. She expected him to be on time and could not afford to keep him waiting on account of her not being ready. It had been awhile since she had last been on date with a guy. She was feeling a little nervous. She looked at her herself in the mirror, and applied her makeup. She put on her favorite red dress and brushed her hair. It was important for her to look her best.
