The big day had finally come. At last, Maria was to receive her degree up on a stage at her university. She was as excited as she was nervous. Mike tried helping Maria get over her stage fright by telling her to picture everybody in the audience wearing only underwear. "This is no time for jokes, Mike. I'm graduating in seven hours," she said, sounding almost a little scared. "I have to get ready," she said. She walked upstairs to change, and Mike followed to get dressed as well.
