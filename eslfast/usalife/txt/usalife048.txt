Mike sat next to Maria in his car. He had finally asked Maria the question that had been on his mind for weeks. Mike had asked Maria to marry him. It hadn't gone as planned, mainly because he never really came up with a plan. Mike had wanted to pop the question to Maria somewhere special, but changed when Maria figured out his plan - or the lack of one. When Maria answered yes, the two of them were parked on a street across from a liquor store.
