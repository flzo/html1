Dr. Doris Thompson woke up to the ringing of her watch. She rolled out of bed. As the sun cast its first rays over the hospital, Doris hurried through the quiet corridors, ready to face whatever challenges awaited her.

The day unfolded in a mix of consultations, diagnoses, and empathetic conversations. In Room 203, she reassured a young girl with a teddy bear in hand, explaining the importance of taking her medicine. In the emergency room, she worked alongside a dedicated team to stabilize a patient in critical condition. In her office, Doris listened to the concerns of an elderly man, offering both medical expertise and a comforting presence. A quick lunch was interrupted by an urgent call, leading her to the operating room for an unexpected surgery.

Exhausted but fulfilled, Dr. Thompson walked out of the hospital at dusk. Another day had passed, leaving a trail of healing and hope in its wake. She couldn't help but reflect on the profound privilege of being a doctor, who bridged the gap between sickness and health.
