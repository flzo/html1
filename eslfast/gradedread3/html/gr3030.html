Mark was notorious for being a bad driver. Every time he got behind the wheel, chaos ensued. His friends had long given up on ever letting him drive during road trips. One sunny afternoon, Mark's friend Amanda reluctantly handed him the car keys. Within minutes, he managed to cut off two cars while merging onto the highway. He ignored stop signs and red lights, prompting angry honks from fellow drivers.

Mark's parking skills were equally dreadful. At the beach, he attempted to park between two cars but ended up bumping both vehicles. An angry crowd gathered. Mark was blamed for his recklessness. Back on the road, Mark couldn't maintain a steady speed. He constantly alternated between speeding and crawling, creating a frustrating and dangerous situation for everyone on the road. Amanda watched in disbelief, regretting her decision

When they finally arrived back home, Amanda vowed never to let Mark drive again. He was indeed a good example of a bad driver, causing stress, annoyance, and danger to everyone on the road.
