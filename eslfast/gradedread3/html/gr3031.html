In a dimly lit basement, a group of friends gathered for their weekly poker night. John, the host, shuffled the deck, his eyes gleaming mischievously. As the dealer, he dealt the cards to the players: Mike, Carolyn, and Mark. The chips clinked as they piled up in front of each player, forming mountains of hope and uncertainty.

The game began with subtle bets, cautious glances, and the occasional nervous laughter. Mark, an optimist, couldn't help but grin. and the tension around the table skyrocketed. The other players exchanged knowing glances and decided to call. The next card revealed a queen, making Carolyn's heart race. She hesitated but then raised the stakes, pushing her chips forward.

With the final card laid down, a collective gasp filled the room. John finally raised the stakes dramatically. In the end, Mark folded, Carolyn called, and Mike decided to go all in. The room was electric with anticipation as they revealed their hands. Amidst cheers and groans, the chips exchanged hands. The game continued into the night.
