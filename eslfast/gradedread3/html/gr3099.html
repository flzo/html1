On the morning of her 18th birthday, Katherine awoke to a quiet house. She tiptoed downstairs, but found only a note on the kitchen table. "Your adventure begins at the place you love most. Head to the place where we've celebrated every milestone. - Mom and Dad." Katherine's heart raced with excitement as she dashed to the family's favorite cafe.

The cafe owner, Mr. Lopez, presented her with a note: "Where the stars meet the sea, your surprise awaits." Katherine knew instantly it meant the cliffside overlooking the ocean, where she had spent countless evenings stargazing. Reaching the cliffside, Katherine gasped. Her friends and family emerged from their hiding spots, shouting, "Surprise!" Balloons and streamers adorned the area; a banner read, "Welcome to Adulthood, Katherine!" Her parents hugged her, eyes glistening with pride.

As the sun set, painting the sky in hues of orange and pink, Katherine realized the true gift wasn't the surprise party; it was the love of those around her. Embracing her new chapter, she made a wish, blew out her candles, and stepped into adulthood surrounded by love.
