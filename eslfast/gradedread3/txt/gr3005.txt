Jessica had always been close to her dad, ever since her mom passed away. Jessica's dad, John, was kind and caring. When he introduced his new girlfriend, Emily, into their lives, Jessica couldn't help but feel a mixture of emotions.
One day, Jessica decided to talk to her dad about Emily. They sat on the porch swing, gently swaying back and forth. "Dad, can I ask you something?" Jessica said. John smiled, his eyes filled with warmth. "Of course, sweetheart, you can ask me anything." Jessica hesitated for a moment, then continued, "I've noticed that you and Emily seem really happy together. She's nice, but I just wanted to make sure that you'll still have time for me." John hugged Jessica. "Jessica, I love you more than anything in this world. You'll always be my number one priority. Our bond is unbreakable."
Jessica felt reassured and smiled. "Okay, Dad, I trust you." John kissed her on the forehead. "Thank you for being so understanding, Jessica. We're going to create even more beautiful memories together as a family."
