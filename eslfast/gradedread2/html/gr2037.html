A family was spending the weekend at the beach. The sun was shining, the sky was blue, and the waves were crashing onto the shore. The children ran towards the water, jumping over the waves as they splashed and played. The parents set up a beach umbrella and chairs, and laid out towels on the sand. As the day went on, they built sandcastles, collected seashells, and went for a walk along the shoreline. They even spotted some dolphins swimming in the distance. When it was time to go, they shook the sand out of their towels, packed up their things, and headed back to the car. The children fell asleep as they drove home.
