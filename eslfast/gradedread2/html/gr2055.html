Mrs. Smith was a teacher at a school. Every morning, she would wake up early, put on her favorite dress, and go to her classroom with a big smile. In her classroom, there were many children eager to learn. Mrs. Smith loved teaching them. She taught them how to read, write, and do math. She also taught them about the world and how to be good friends. One day, the children surprised her with colorful drawings and cards. They said, "Thank you, Mrs. Smith, for being a wonderful teacher." She felt so happy and touched by their kindness. At the end of the school year, Mrs. Smith felt proud of her job as a teacher. She knew that she was making a positive difference in the lives of her students.
