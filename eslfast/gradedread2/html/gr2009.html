Patricia was about to turn 6 years old. Her parents wanted to do something special for her birthday. The day before her birthday, her parents decorated the living room with balloons, streamers, and a big banner that said "Happy Birthday, Patricia!" They also baked a delicious chocolate cake. On the day of Patricia's birthday, her parents took her out for a walk in the park while their friends and family arrived at their house. When they returned home, Patricia's parents blindfolded her and led her into the living room. When they removed the blindfold, Patricia was completely surprised to see all her loved ones gathered in the room, yelling "surprise!" It was a wonderful day for Patricia, full of love, and happiness.
