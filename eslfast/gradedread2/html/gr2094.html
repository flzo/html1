Today was a special day - Mother's Day! Michael wanted to make it the best day ever for his mom. In the morning, he tiptoed into the kitchen and prepared breakfast: toast with butter and jam, and a glass of orange juice. He even tried to make a heart shape with the jam. "Surprise, Mom!" Michael exclaimed as he carried the tray to her. Mom's face lit up with a big smile. "Thank you, Michael. It looks delicious!" After breakfast, they went for a walk in the park. In the afternoon, they made a special card together. Michael drew a picture of him and his mom holding hands. At dinner, they ordered pizza and had ice cream for dessert. Mom didn't have to cook or clean - it was her day to relax.
