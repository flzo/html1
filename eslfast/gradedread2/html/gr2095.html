Today was Father's Day! Jenny wanted to make her dad feel loved and appreciated. In the morning, she made her dad's favorite pancakes. She carefully flipped them, making sure they were just right. "Dad, breakfast is ready!" Jenny called, carrying the pancakes to the table. Dad smiled. "Wow, thank you, Jenny." After breakfast, they went for a bike ride in the park. Jenny and her dad pedaled through the trees, feeling the warm sun on their faces. In the afternoon, they worked on a craft project together. They made a special card with colorful paper and glitter. Jenny wrote on it, "To the World's Greatest Dad" At dinner, they cooked burgers on the grill. Before bedtime, Jenny gave her dad a big, tight hug. "I love you, Dad," she said.
