Lily wanted to send some money to her grandma. She loved her grandma so much. Her mom taught her how to send money using her computer. They went to a website called "Money Movers" and created an account. Lily's mom showed her how to enter her grandma's name and bank account information, and how much money she wanted to send. Lily was a little nervous, but her mom assured her that it was easy and safe. They clicked a button, and the money was sent to grandma's bank account. A few days later, Lily's grandma called and thanked her for the money. Grandma was very happy and said she could buy some special treats because of Lily's help. Lily felt proud of herself for knowing how to send money.
