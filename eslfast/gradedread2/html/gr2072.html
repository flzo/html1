Lily wanted to surprise her grandma with some beautiful flowers for her birthday. She knew her grandma loved flowers. Lily went to the flower shop with her mom. There were so many colorful flowers! She saw red roses, yellow daisies, and purple tulips. A friendly florist came over and asked, "Who are you buying the flowers for, dear?" Lily told her that she was buying flowers for her grandma. The florist showed Lily some lovely blue hydrangeas. Lily's eyes lit up. She knew those were the perfect flowers for her grandma. Lily's grandma was delighted when she received the beautiful flowers. She said, "You chose the best flowers, my dear. I love them!"
