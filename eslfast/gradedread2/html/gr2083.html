Mia dreamed of dancing. She saw dancers on TV and wanted to be just like them. Mia asked her mom, "Can I learn to dance, please?" Mom smiled and said, "Of course, Mia! Let's find a dance class for you." They found a nearby dance studio and signed up. Mia was excited and a little nervous on her first day. In class, the teacher taught them to move to the music. Mia learned to step, twirl, and jump. It was fun! She practiced at home, too, using her favorite songs. Months passed. Mia's dancing improved. She felt more confident. One day, the dance class had a performance. Mia wore a beautiful costume and danced on a big stage. Her family clapped and cheered for her.
