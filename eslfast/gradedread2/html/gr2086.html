Timmy always dreamed of playing the guitar. One afternoon, Timmy's neighbor, Mr. Johnson, a skilled guitarist, saw Timmy gazing at his guitar longingly. Mr. Johnson offered to give him a guitar lesson. Timmy's eyes lit up with excitement. Mr. Johnson began with the basics. He explained the different parts of the guitar and how to hold it properly. Then, he taught Timmy a simple chord, the "G" chord. Timmy's fingers fumbled at first, but he kept trying. With patience and encouragement, Mr. Johnson and Timmy strummed the guitar together. Slowly but surely, Timmy's fingers found their place on the strings, and the sound began to harmonize. From that day forward, Timmy practiced tirelessly. His guitar skills improved.
