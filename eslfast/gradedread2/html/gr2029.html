Her best friend's birthday was coming up. Sharon wanted to make sure her friend knew how much she cared, so she decided to send her a birthday card. She went to the store, bought a nice card. She then took it home and wrote a heartfelt message inside, telling her friend how much she meant to her and wishing her a happy birthday. She carefully addressed the envelope and put the card inside. The next day, she went to the post office and mailed it. A few days later, Sharon's friend received the card. She opened it and read the message with a big smile on her face.
