In a quiet neighborhood, there was a small house with a beautiful garden. Inside the house lived a family of four: Mom, Dad, Sarah, and Tim. One sunny afternoon, Sarah and Tim were playing catch in the backyard. Tim threw the ball too high, and it flew right into the living room window with a loud crash. The window broke into tiny pieces! Mom and Dad rushed outside. They saw the broken window and asked, "Are you two okay?" Sarah and Tim felt scared. They said sorry, and told their parents about the accident. Dad hugged them and said, "Accidents happen. We're just glad you're okay." Together, they cleaned up the broken glass. Sarah and Tim learned to be more careful when playing near the house.
