Nancy was going to try online shopping. She opened her computer, went to a website, and saw so many pretty dresses and shoes. She clicked on a beautiful blue dress she really liked. To buy the dress, Nancy had to add it to her "cart" and then "checkout." She also had to enter her address and payment information. A few days later, a package arrived at her doorstep. It was her dress! Nancy tried it on; it fit perfectly. She was so happy with her online shopping experience. From then on, Nancy enjoyed shopping online for clothes and other things. She even helped her mom with online grocery shopping. It was a modern way to shop, and Nancy loved it.
