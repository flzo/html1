John had a job at a big office. He was usually a good worker, but one day, everything went wrong. He woke up late because his alarm clock didn't go off. He rushed to get dressed and didn't have time for breakfast. When he got to work, his boss asked for an important report he was supposed to finish, but he had forgotten all about it. During a meeting, John spilled coffee on his boss's important documents, and everyone in the room stared at him. He felt so embarrassed. Later, he tried to fix the office printer, but instead, he made it stop working altogether. By the end of the day, John was exhausted and feeling like he was the worst employee in the world.
