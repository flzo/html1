Sandra loved eating Chinese food, but she always struggled with using chopsticks. One day, her parents took her to a Chinese restaurant for lunch. Sandra was excited, but worried about embarrassing herself. Her mom handed her a pair of chopsticks and said, "Don't worry, Sandra, we'll help you." Sandra watched her parents and tried to mimic their movements. It was tricky at first. The slippery dumpling slipped from her grasp, and kept falling back onto her plate. But Sandra didn't give up. Her determination paid off. With patience and practice, Sandra began to improve. Soon she was able to pick up dumplings with chopsticks. Sandra was no longer afraid of using chopsticks.
