Barbara can bake delicious cookies. One day, while enjoying her homemade cookies at a picnic, Barbara had an idea. "Why not share these tasty cookies with everyone?" she thought. Barbara decided to start a cookie business. She rented a small stall at the local farmers' market. On the first day, the aroma of freshly baked cookies drew in customers. People tried her cookies and liked them. Word quickly spread, and soon, Barbara's Sweet Delights became the talk of the town. As her business grew, Barbara hired a few helpers to keep up with demand. She even began selling them online. Barbara's dream had turned into a successful business, all because of her delicious idea and hard work.
