Schools were closed due to a virus outbreak. Students had to continue their studies online at home. At first, the kids were excited about learning from home with no need to wake up early. But soon they realized that online learning was not easy. It was difficult to concentrate on their studies while sitting in front of a computer screen for hours. There were also many distractions, such as parents talking on the phone, and pets running around. The online classes were not as interactive as their regular classes. They missed their friends and teachers. Despite the challenges, the kids learned to manage their time better. They learned how to use various online tools such as video conferencing, chat rooms, and virtual whiteboards.
