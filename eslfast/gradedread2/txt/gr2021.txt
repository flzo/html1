Olivia lived in the city. She had always wanted to visit a farm. One summer, her parents decided to take her on a trip to a farm. As they drove down the winding country roads, Olivia looked out of the window at the green fields. When they arrived at the farm, Olivia was surprised by how different it was from her city home. There were cows in the fields, chickens around the yard, and horses in the paddocks. The farmer greeted them warmly and showed them around the farm. Olivia was happy to see the animals up close. She even helped gather eggs and feed the cows. At the end of the day, Olivia's parents thanked the farmer for the unforgettable experience.
