Betty was a little girl. She loved animals. One sunny day, her parents took her to a pet store to look at all the cute animals. As soon as they entered the store, Betty's eyes widened with excitement. She saw fluffy kittens, colorful birds, and playful puppies. She walked around the store, admiring each animal she saw. In the corner of the store was a tank full of tropical fish. Betty had never seen so many beautiful fish before. Finally, Betty's parents said it was time to go home. As they walked out, Betty's parents asked her which animal she liked the most. Betty thought for a moment and said, "I love them all!"
