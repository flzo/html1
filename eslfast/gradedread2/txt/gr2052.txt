Alex was 16 years old. He wanted to have a job and earn some money. After searching online for a while, Alex found a good job. He became a dog walker. Every day after school, Alex would put on comfortable shoes and head to the nearby park. There, many dog owners needed help walking their furry friends. Alex loved spending time with the dogs. They all wagged their tails and made Alex smile. The job was fun. Alex got to enjoy the fresh air, exercise, and the company of friendly dogs. It was a great way to earn some pocket money. As time went on, Alex learned about different dog breeds, their personalities, and how to take care of them. It was a job that didn't feel like work at all.
