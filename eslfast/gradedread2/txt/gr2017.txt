Sophie was a bright and curious seven-year-old girl. One day, her parents told her that they were going to move to a new home. Sophie had never moved before, and the idea of leaving her friends and school made her feel sad. But her parents explained that they needed to move closer to her dad's new job, and that the new house was bigger and nicer than their old one. On the day of the move, Sophie sat in the back seat of the car watching their old house disappear from view. When they arrived at the new house, Sophie felt excited. The house was really bigger and nicer, with a big backyard.
