One sunny morning, Sarah's mom took her to the supermarket. They needed to buy groceries for the week. Inside the supermarket, they found rows and rows of shelves filled with food. Mom pushed a shopping cart. First, they went to the fruit and vegetable section. They picked out juicy apples, sweet bananas, and fresh lettuce. Next, they headed to the bread aisle. Sarah loved the smell of freshly baked bread. They chose a loaf of soft, delicious bread for sandwiches. As they walked through the store, they added milk, eggs, and cheese to their cart. Sarah helped by putting things in the cart carefully. At the checkout counter, the cashier scanned their items, and Mom paid. They left the supermarket with bags full of groceries.
