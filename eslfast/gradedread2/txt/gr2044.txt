Margaret loved her garden. She would spend every spare moment in the garden, pulling weeds, planting new seeds, and watering. One sunny afternoon, as she watered the rosebush, Margaret noticed that her tomatoes were starting to grow big and juicy. She smiled with pride, knowing that soon she would be able to harvest them and use them to make delicious homemade salsa. While she was working, she heard a sound coming from the bushes. It was a little bunny. Margaret laughed and gently shooed it away. As the sun began to set, Margaret wiped the sweat from her forehead. Her garden was thriving. She felt a sense of peace and contentment.
