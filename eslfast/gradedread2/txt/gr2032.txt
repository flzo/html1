Christmas was coming, but Linda's father just lost his job. Money was tight in the family. Linda didn't want to ask for anything for Christmas. Instead, she decided to make a special gift for her parents. For weeks, Linda worked hard on her gift. On Christmas morning, Linda handed her parents her gift. It was a small, handmade photo album filled with pictures of their family, memories, and moments they had shared together. As her parents flipped through the pages, tears welled up in their eyes. They hugged Linda tightly, telling her how much they loved her and were proud of her. Linda knew that she had given her parents the best gift of all - the gift of love, memories, and gratitude.
