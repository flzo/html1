The Smith family had been saving up for months to buy a new car. One Saturday morning, they went to a car dealership. They were greeted by a friendly salesperson, who asked them what they were looking for. Mr. Smith explained that they needed a car that was reliable, fuel-efficient, and could fit their family of six. The salesperson showed them a few different options, and they took each one for a test drive. They finally settled on a bright red SUV that had all the features they were looking for. The Smiths signed the papers and drove off the lot in their new car. The kids were thrilled with the extra space in the back seat.
