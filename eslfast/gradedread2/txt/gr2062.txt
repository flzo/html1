Tim took the bus to school every day. The bus was big and yellow. Mr. Johnson is a friendly driver. One sunny morning, as Tim got on the bus, he noticed something unusual. There was a tiny kitten sitting in the backseat! Tim was surprised and excited. He asked the driver, "Mr. Johnson, is that a kitten on the bus?" Mr. Johnson smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. It seems this little kitten wanted to go on an adventure today." Tim gently picked up the kitten and held it in his arms. He knew he had to help the kitten find its home. After school, Tim took the kitten to the animal shelter. A kind lady there said she would find a loving family for the kitten.
