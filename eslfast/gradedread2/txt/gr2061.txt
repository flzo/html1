Mr. Johnson was friendly, but he was a noisy neighbor. Every morning, his alarm clock would ring loudly, waking up everyone in the building. He would then play his music with the volume turned up very high. In the evenings, Mr. Johnson loved to practice playing his drums. The neighbors would often knock on his door and ask him to keep it down, but he seemed to forget quickly. One day, the neighbors decided to have a friendly talk with him. They explained how his noise was causing them trouble. From that day on, Mr. Johnson used headphones when listening to music, and he played his drums during reasonable hours. The neighbors were relieved. The neighborhood was once again a peaceful place to live.
