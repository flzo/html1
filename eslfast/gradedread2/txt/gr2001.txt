John is an ordinary American man who lives in a small town in the Midwest. He wakes up at 6:30 AM every day. After getting out of bed, he brushes his teeth, takes a quick shower, and gets dressed in his work clothes. At 7:00 AM, John goes downstairs to the kitchen to make himself breakfast. He usually has a bowl of cereal with milk and two slices of bread. While he eats, he checks his phone to catch up on any news or messages he may have missed overnight. After breakfast, John heads out the door and drives to work.
