Carol was a teenager. One evening, she was asked to babysit for a family in her neighborhood. The family had a 4-year-old boy named Jack. Carol arrived at the house and met Jack and his parents. They showed her around the house and gave her some instructions. Carol played games and read books with Jack. They also made some cookies together which they enjoyed as a snack. When it was time for bed, Carol read Jack a bedtime story. After Jack fell asleep, Carol spent some time cleaning up and doing some light chores around the house. When the parents returned home, they were happy to see that everything was in order and that Jack had had a great time with Carol.
