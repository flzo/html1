Emma wanted to learn about online banking. She had heard that it was a convenient way to manage her money. She decided to visit her local bank to get started. A friendly bank teller named Lisa greeted her. Lisa helped Emma create an online banking account. Emma chose a username and a strong password to keep her account safe. Lisa explained, "With online banking, you can check your balance, pay bills, and even transfer money to your friends and family." Lisa showed her how to log in to her account on the bank's website. Emma saw her balance and how much money she had in her savings account. Emma practiced making a small bill payment right there in the bank. It was so easy!
