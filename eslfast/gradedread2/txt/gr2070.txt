Michelle, Ben, and Mia attended the same school. Every day, they used to go to school separately, each in their own car. Michelle's mom suggested carpooling to school. Carpooling meant they could take turns riding in one car, which would save gas. The kids loved the idea! The next day, it was Ben's turn to pick up Michelle and Mia. They all hopped into Ben's car with big smiles on their faces. They chatted, sang songs, and even played some fun games in the car. Carpooling not only saved their money on gas, but also gave the kids more time to be with their friends. They laughed and talked, making the morning ride to school much more enjoyable.
