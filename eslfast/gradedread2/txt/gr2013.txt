Kevin lived in a small house with his parents. He always wanted a dog to play with, but his parents said they didn't have enough space or time to take care of one. One day, Kevin was walking home from school, he found a small puppy that looked lost and scared. Kevin picked up the puppy and took him home. Kevin's parents were surprised when they saw the puppy, but they saw how happy Kevin was and agreed to let him keep the dog. Kevin named the puppy Buddy, and they quickly became best friends. They did everything together. Buddy made Kevin feel happy. He couldn't imagine his life without Buddy.
