Ben loved spending his weekends at his grandma's house. It was a cozy cottage with a big garden full of colorful flowers. Every Saturday morning, Ben's parents would drive him over. Ben would run outside to explore the garden. He loved picking fresh strawberries and playing with Grandma's cat. Grandma was an excellent cook. She would make Ben's favorite pancakes for breakfast. They would spend the day painting pictures, playing board games, and going for walks. On Saturday night, Grandma would make a delicious dinner. They would watch old movies together. On Sunday morning, Grandma would take Ben to the nearby pond to feed the ducks. Ben cherished these wonderful memories at Grandma's house.
