A family named the Martins moved into a new house. They were excited about their new home and decided to have a housewarming party. They sent out invitations to their neighbors and friends. On the day of the party, the Martins decorated their house with balloons and streamers. They had a big table full of delicious food, like sandwiches, pizza, and cake. As the guests arrived, they brought thoughtful gifts for the Martins, like potted plants and cozy blankets. The children played games in the backyard, while the adults chatted and laughed inside. Mr. and Mrs. Martin gave tours of their new home, showing off their spacious living room and beautiful garden. Everyone admired the house and felt happy for the Martins.
