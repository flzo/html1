Emma wanted to go to the park, but it was too far to walk. She needed a ride. She asked her dad, "Can I get a ride to the park, please?" Dad smiled and said, "Of course, Emma! Let's go." They went to the garage. Emma hopped into the back seat and buckled her seatbelt. Dad started the car, and they drove to the park. Along the way, they listened to their favorite songs on the radio. When they reached the park, Emma jumped out. She thanked Dad for the ride and promised to have fun and be safe. At the park, Emma played with her friends. She had a wonderful time. When it was time to go home, Dad was already there to give her another ride.
