Sam was very rich. He had a big house, fancy cars, and a lot of money in the bank, but Sam was not happy. Every morning, he woke up to his luxurious life, but something was missing. He spent his days making even more money, but it didn't bring him joy. One day, while taking a walk in a beautiful park, Sam met a little girl named Lily with her family. He saw how they cherished their time together, even with less money. Sam realized that money couldn't buy happiness. He decided to have a simple life, spend more time with loved ones, and enjoy the little things. As he did, his heart filled with happiness. Sam learned that happiness came from the heart, not from his bank account.
