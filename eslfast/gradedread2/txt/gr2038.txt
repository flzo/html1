Emily loved music. One day, her parents surprised her with a piano. She was excited and started to learn how to play it. At first, Emily struggled to remember where each note was on the keyboard. She tried playing a few simple songs, but her fingers wouldn't cooperate. Emily felt discouraged. But her parents encouraged her to keep practicing. Emily continued to practice every day. Slowly but surely, she started to improve. She was able to play a few notes together and then a short melody. With each passing day, she grew more confident and excited to play. After many months of practice, Emily was able to play a full song. Her parents cheered with excitement.
