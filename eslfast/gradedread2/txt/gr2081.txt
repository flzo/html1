There was a big dinner party at Lisa's house. She invited her friends and family. Everyone was excited. Lisa's mom, Mrs. Johnson, was busy in the kitchen. She cooked delicious food like spaghetti, pizza, and chocolate cake. The guests arrived and sat at the long table. They talked and laughed while waiting for dinner. When the food was ready, Mrs. Johnson brought out the dishes. The table was full of yummy dishes. As they ate, Lisa's dad told funny stories, and her little brother, Timmy, entertained everyone with his silly jokes. After dinner, Mrs. Johnson brought out a big chocolate cake with candles. It was Lisa's birthday! Her friends and family all stood up singing "Happy Birthday" to her. The dinner party was a huge success.
