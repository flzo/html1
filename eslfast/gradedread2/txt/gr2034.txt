Lisa loved to cook, but she didn't enjoy washing the dishes. Every time she finished cooking, the pile of dirty dishes would stare back at her, waiting to be cleaned. One day, Lisa's mom told her to keep the kitchen clean. Lisa decided to make washing dishes fun. She put on her favorite music and started to wash the dishes one by one, feeling the warm water and soap bubbles on her hands. As she finished each dish, she felt a sense of accomplishment. Lisa realized that washing dishes was not as bad as she thought, and that it could even be enjoyable. From then on, She made sure to wash her dishes right after cooking and to have fun with it.
