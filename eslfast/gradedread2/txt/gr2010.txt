Jack was feeling hungry, He decided to make a sandwich for himself. He went to the kitchen and opened the refrigerator. He took out a loaf of bread, some cheese, lettuce, and turkey slices. He placed two slices of bread on a plate and spread some mayonnaise on them. Next, he placed some cheese slices on one slice of bread, followed by the lettuce and turkey slices. He then placed the other slice of bread on top of the filling to complete the sandwich. Jack took a big bite of his sandwich. It's delicious! He felt very proud of himself for making the sandwich all by himself.
