Sam was going on his first airplane ride. He was filled with excitement as he entered the big airport with his family. They boarded their plane. Sam got a window seat. As the plane took off, Sam looked out the window. The world below became tiny, like a toy town, and the clouds were like fluffy pillows. During the flight, the flight attendants served snacks and drinks. Sam got a little bag of pretzels and a juice box. He looked out the window again and saw the sun setting in the sky. It was like a painting of red and orange. After a while, the plane started to descend. Sam's excitement returned as they landed safely. He enjoyed the first airplane ride.
