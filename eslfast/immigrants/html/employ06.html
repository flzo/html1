Once a person gets the job he or she wants, it then becomes important to be a good employee to the organization. The new employee should be a valuable addition to the team. There are many ways a person can be considered a good employee. Being prompt is a very important aspect of any job. If a person's working hours are from 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., for instance, it would be wise to be on time or a few minutes earlier. Allowing extra time for your trip to the office is always a good idea. Unexpected delays such as traffic accidents or foul weather can all cause you to be late. Commuter buses and trains could be delayed, too.  

Being late is never a good idea, but it is also important to be at work and ready to perform your duties every day. Most jobs still adhere to a five-day schedule from Monday through Friday. There are many exceptions depending on the type of position you hold, but again, most people work a standard five-day work week. Some other things a worker can do to be a good employee is to have a positive attitude about your job. An enthusiastic employee is a great asset to the employer. Dressing properly, being trustworthy, following the organization's rules all help you become a good employee.

One of the best things a person can have is to find a job that he or she really enjoys. There is an old saying that reads, "If you love what you do, you'll never work a day in your life." Loving your job will be rewarding, satisfying, and it will give you a great attitude.
