Bright sunlight streamed through the office windows as Claire, the hiring manager at GreenTech Solutions, prepared for a busy day of interviews. The company had a job vacancy for a project coordinator. Today, they were meeting several candidates.

The first candidate, Mark, arrived promptly at 9 a.m. Claire greeted him with a smile and invited him to sit down. "Tell me about your experience," Claire began. Mark launched into a detailed description of his previous job, achievements, and skills. Claire was impressed. However, as the interview progressed, it became clear that Mark was more focused on his own accomplishments than on teamwork, which were crucial for the role.

Next was Monica, who arrived slightly late. Claire reminded herself not to judge too quickly. Monica apologized for the delay. Despite the rocky start, Monica quickly won Claire over with her genuine enthusiasm and practical examples of problem-solving in her previous job. She spoke about leading a small team to complete a project under a tight deadline, emphasizing her collaborative approach and adaptability.

Finally, there was Daniel, who had a calm demeanor and a background in a different industry. Claire was initially unsure if he would be a good fit, but as the interview went on, Daniel shared how his unique experiences could bring a fresh perspective to the team. He talked about his passion for sustainable practices, which aligned perfectly with GreenTech Solutions' mission.

After the interviews, Claire sat back and reviewed her notes. A while later, she made her decision. She picked up the phone and called Monica. "We'd like to offer you the position," she said warmly. Monica's delighted response confirmed that Claire had made the right choice.
