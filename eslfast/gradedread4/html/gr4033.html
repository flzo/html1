Samantha woke up early on her first day at Weston College. The sun streamed through her dorm room window, casting a warm glow on the neatly arranged books and decorations. She took a deep breath. Today marked the beginning of a new chapter. Dressed in her favorite outfit, Samantha grabbed her backpack and headed out.

The campus was alive with activities; students were chatting, hurrying to their classes. Samantha's first class was English Literature. She walked into the classroom, her heart pounding. Finding a seat near the front, she smiled at a girl sitting next to her. "Hi, I'm Samantha." "Hi! I'm Rachel," the girl replied with a friendly grin. They chatted briefly before the professor entered. Samantha felt a bit more at ease knowing she'd made a new friend. The professor, Dr. Matthews, had a warm demeanor and a passion for literature. As he discussed the syllabus, Samantha's excitement grew.

After class, Samantha and Rachel explored the campus together. They wandered through the student union, where various clubs and organizations had set up tables. Lunchtime found them at the bustling cafeteria. Samantha marveled at the variety of food options. She and Rachel found a spot at a table where a few other freshmen were sitting. They quickly struck up conversations, sharing their backgrounds and interests. The afternoon was filled with more classes.

By the end of the day, Samantha felt a sense of belonging. She loved the vibrant energy of Weston College and the friendly faces she'd encountered. Her first day had been everything she'd hoped for and more. Lying in bed that night, she couldn't wait to see what the rest of the semester would bring. Weston College already felt like home.
