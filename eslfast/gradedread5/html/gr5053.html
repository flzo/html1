Biotechnology is the use of living things, like cells and bacteria, to make useful products or solve problems. This science helps in many parts of life. People use biotechnology in medicine, agriculture, industry, and the environment.

In medicine, biotechnology helps doctors treat diseases. Scientists can make medicines, vaccines, and even special cells to cure illnesses. For example, insulin for people with diabetes is made using bacteria. Gene therapy, which changes genes in the body, is also possible because of biotechnology. This can help treat some genetic diseases.  

In agriculture, biotechnology makes plants stronger and more productive. Scientists can create crops that resist insects, diseases, or bad weather. This helps farmers grow more food with fewer chemicals. Biotechnology also helps improve the quality of food. For example, rice or corn can be enriched with vitamins to fight malnutrition.

In industry, biotechnology helps make products in a clean and safe way. Microorganisms can produce biofuels, enzymes, and chemicals for daily use. This process is often better for the environment than traditional methods because it can use less energy and produce less waste.

Biotechnology is also important for the environment. Scientists use bacteria and plants to clean polluted water, soil, or air. This process is called bioremediation. Biotechnology can also help protect endangered species by supporting breeding programs or preserving genetic material.

In conclusion, biotechnology has many important applications. It improves health, food, industry, and the environment. It helps people solve problems in smart and safe ways. As technology grows, biotechnology will continue to make life better for everyone.
