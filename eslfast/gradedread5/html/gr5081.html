For a long time, only astronauts could travel to space. They trained for many years and went on special missions to explore the universe. Today, things are beginning to change. Space travel is no longer just for professionals. Ordinary people may soon have the chance to experience it too.

Several private companies are working on space tourism. These companies design rockets and spacecraft that can carry passengers safely. Instead of years of training, tourists only need to learn some basic safety rules. The goal is to make space travel exciting but also safe and comfortable.  

Right now, space tourism is very expensive. A ticket can cost millions of dollars, so only very rich people can go. However, just like airplanes in the past, the price may become cheaper over time. In the early days of flight, only a few people could afford it. Today, millions of people fly every day. The same thing might happen with space travel in the future.

What will tourists experience? First, they will see Earth from above. From space, our planet looks like a beautiful blue and white ball floating in darkness. Passengers will also feel weightless for a few minutes, just like astronauts. This is called "zero gravity," and it makes people float inside the spacecraft.

Some companies even plan to build space hotels. Visitors might stay for a few days, enjoy the view of Earth, and feel what it is like to live in space.

Space travel for ordinary people is still new, but it is growing quickly. In the future, more and more people may get the chance to explore beyond our planet. It could become one of the greatest adventures of human life.
