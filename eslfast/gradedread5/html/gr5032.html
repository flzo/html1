Renewable energy is energy that comes from natural sources that do not run out. These sources can make energy again and again, so people can use them for a long time. Unlike oil, coal, or gas, renewable energy is clean and better for the environment. It does not create as much pollution, and it helps reduce climate change.

One common type of renewable energy is solar energy. Solar panels collect light from the sun and turn it into electricity. Many homes and businesses use solar panels to lower their energy bills. Another type is wind energy. Big wind turbines use the power of the wind to make electricity. Wind farms, which have many turbines, are often built in open areas or near the sea.  

Hydropower is also renewable energy. It uses the movement of water in rivers or dams to produce electricity. Some countries, like Norway, get most of their electricity from hydropower. Another example is geothermal energy, which comes from heat inside the earth. This energy can be used to make electricity or to heat buildings. Finally, biomass energy comes from plants, wood, or even waste materials. These things can be burned or changed into fuel.

Renewable energy is important because it helps us use less fossil fuel. Fossil fuels are limited and will run out one day, but renewable sources will always be here. They also create less air pollution, which means cleaner air and a healthier planet.

In the future, more countries will use renewable energy to meet their power needs. It will take time and money to build the technology, but it is worth it. Renewable energy is the key to a cleaner and safer world.
