Hydropower is one of the most common forms of renewable energy in the world. It uses the movement of water to create electricity. For thousands of years, people have used water to turn wheels for grinding grain or running simple machines. Today, we use modern technology to capture the power of water in a much bigger way.

Hydropower usually comes from dams built on rivers. A dam holds back water in a large reservoir. When the water is released, it flows through turbines, which are machines with blades that spin. As the turbines spin, they turn generators, and the generators produce electricity. This process is clean because it does not burn fuel or release harmful gases into the air.  

One of the biggest advantages of hydropower is that it is renewable. The water cycle is natural and endless. Rain and snow refill rivers and lakes, so the supply of water keeps coming. Hydropower is also reliable, because engineers can control how much water passes through the turbines. This makes it easier to provide a steady supply of electricity compared to some other renewable sources, such as solar or wind.

However, hydropower also has some challenges. Building a dam is very expensive and takes a lot of time. Dams can also affect the environment. For example, fish and other animals may find it hard to move freely in rivers. Large reservoirs can even flood villages and farmland.

Even with these problems, hydropower is an important part of the world's energy supply. It provides clean electricity to millions of people. As countries look for ways to reduce pollution and fight climate change, hydropower will continue to play an important role in creating a greener future.
