In recent years, China has been developing what is called the "low-altitude economy." This term may sound new, but it is easy to understand. The low-altitude economy means using the airspace below 1,000 meters for business, services, and daily life. It is mainly about drones, flying taxis, and small aircraft.
Drones are already common in China. They are used to deliver packages, take aerial photos, and even bring food to people. For example, some cities in China now allow drones to deliver medicine to hospitals quickly, saving important time. This shows how low-altitude technology can make life easier and safer.
Flying taxis are another part of the low-altitude economy. These are small aircraft that can carry people over short distances. In the future, people may be able to take a flying taxi to avoid traffic jams. This idea may sound like science fiction, but companies in China are already testing these vehicles.
The low-altitude economy is not just about transportation. It also supports tourism, emergency rescue, and farming. For example, tourists can enjoy low-altitude sightseeing flights. In farming, drones are used to spray crops and monitor fields, which helps farmers work more efficiently.
The Chinese government sees the low-altitude economy as a new growth area. It is making rules and building systems to support safe flying. Experts believe this industry will create many new jobs and business opportunities.
In short, China's low-altitude economy is changing how people live and work. From drone delivery to air taxis, it may soon become part of everyday life. While there are still challenges like safety and regulations, the future looks exciting. This new economy shows how technology can open the skies for everyone.
