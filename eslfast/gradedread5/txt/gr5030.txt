TSMC, or Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company, is one of the most important technology companies in the world. It was founded in 1987 in Taiwan by Morris Chang. TSMC is not a company that sells products directly to consumers. Instead, it makes computer chips for other companies, such as Apple, AMD, and NVIDIA. These chips are the "brains" inside smartphones, computers, cars, and many other electronic devices.
TSMC is known as a semiconductor foundry, which means it manufactures chips that other companies design. This business model is called pure-play foundry, and TSMC was the first company in the world to do this successfully. Before TSMC, most chip companies both designed and made their own chips. TSMC changed the industry by allowing many companies to focus only on design while TSMC handled the manufacturing.
The company's success comes from its advanced technology and high-quality production. TSMC makes chips that are very small and powerful, using cutting-edge processes measured in nanometers (nm). For example, it produces chips with 3-nanometer technology, which are among the smallest and most efficient in the world.
TSMC plays a key role in the global electronics supply chain. Many industries depend on its chips, from smartphones to electric cars. Because of this, TSMC is sometimes called the "heart of global technology."
The company also helps Taiwan's economy grow and gives the country an important place in the world's technology market. TSMC continues to invest in research and build new factories in Taiwan and other countries, such as the United States.
In short, TSMC is a world leader in chip manufacturing, and its technology powers much of our modern digital world every day.
