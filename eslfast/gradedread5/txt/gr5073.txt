Waymo is a company that makes self-driving cars. You might also hear them called robotaxis. A robotaxi is a car without a human driver. It uses advanced technology to see the world and drive safely.
These cars are very smart. They use cameras, lasers, and radar to "see" everything around them. They can spot other cars, people walking, traffic lights, and stop signs. A powerful computer inside the car acts as the brain. It processes all the information instantly to decide when to go, stop, or turn. This technology is designed to follow all traffic rules and be very careful.
Using a Waymo robotaxi is simple. You use a smartphone app, just like with other ride-sharing services. You enter your destination, and a car arrives to pick you up. The big difference is that the driver's seat is empty. You get in, and the car starts its journey. You can relax, look out the window, or even read a book while the car drives to your place.
Right now, you can find Waymo robotaxis operating in a few American cities, like Phoenix and San Francisco. The company is slowly expanding to new areas.
In conclusion, Waymo represents an exciting step into the future of travel. It shows us a world where cars can drive themselves, potentially making our roads safer and our journeys more convenient. It is a fascinating example of how technology is changing our everyday lives.
