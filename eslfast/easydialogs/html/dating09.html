A: Who did you go out with?

A: Where did you go?

A: Anywhere else?

B: Then we went to a jazz club.

A: That sounds like a nice date.

B: Yes, it was pleasant.

A: But you won't date him again?

B: No. He was nice, but there was no chemistry.
